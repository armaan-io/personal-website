01 / about

Many interests, one thread: curiosity.

I tend to learn by getting my hands involved first. I’ll follow an interest for a while, drift toward something else, then come back with a different way of seeing it. It took me some time to stop treating that movement as a lack of focus. I have started to see it as part of how I work.

I keep finding that ideas travel well. Something learned in one kind of practice can quietly change how I approach another, often in ways that are hard to predict at the start. The more experiences I collect, the more connections I notice between them, and the easier it becomes to find a useful next step. I like that the process stays open: there is always another question, another angle, or something worth trying with my hands.
